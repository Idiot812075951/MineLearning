# Walk Cycle Breakdown

Example timing only. Adapt stride duration and foot mechanics to the reference, character proportions and locomotion speed. For a rigid mecha, keep segment lengths fixed and do not force an organic heel roll where its foot construction cannot support one.

## Contact (Frame 0/12)

- Heel strike on contact foot
- Opposite foot behind, near toe-off (not the passing pose)
- Hips at neutral height
- Arm opposite to leg forward

## Down (Frame 3/15)

- Contact leg bent, weight settling
- Hips lowest point
- Rear foot lifting into swing while the forward leg accepts weight

## Passing (Frame 6/18)

- Support leg approximately under the body; avoid a hard knee lock
- Passing leg mid-swing
- Hips rising

## Up (Frame 9/21)

- Support heel rising as the body moves over the support foot
- Hips highest point
- Swing leg advances toward the next contact; do not confuse this with the next toe-off event

## Cycle Length

24 frames @ 30fps is an illustrative cycle; 16 frames is a faster example, not a universal gait rule. Check support, trajectory and seam velocity in continuous playback. A matching endpoint may be retained for interpolation but must not be played twice in a repeating clip.
